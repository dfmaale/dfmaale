## About Me

I am a statistician and data scientist, currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Applied Science and Technology (Data Science and Analytics) at North Carolina A&T State University. My research lies at the intersection of robust statistical learning, artificial intelligence, healthcare, mental health, and public health.

I develop statistical and machine‑learning methods that remain reliable under real‑world complications such as missing data, distribution shift, model uncertainty, and fairness constraints. Broadly, my work aims to translate rigorous methodology into actionable evidence for research, practice, and policy, ensuring that predictive systems remain trustworthy in the settings where they matter most.

## Research Focus

## Featured Research

### SciGuard: Statistical Assurance Regions

A framework for certifying predictive-model risk under joint missingness and distribution shift. The work studies observed risk, simultaneous upper confidence bands, simultaneous coverage, and assurance-region recall to distinguish operating conditions where model performance is statistically supported.

### Second-Generation *p*-Values

Methodological research involving interval-null inference, confidence-interval overlap, sample-size determination, missing-data mechanisms, multiple imputation, and classifier performance.

### Predictive Modeling with Missing Health Data

Research examining how imputation choices affect supervised machine-learning performance in health-data classification.
